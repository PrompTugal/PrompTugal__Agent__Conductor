## The post's argument (Dinis's words, from the screenshot)
"Here is another perfect example of RiskMandate's Agent Behaviour policies), which nicely solves the problem of putting a message on the wrong Claude session tab (which is also a self 'prompt injection' attack). In this case (as you can see) the 'outreach' agent, who has a very specific brief and tight ABP, just said: 'Nothing to do with me, and even if I tried I couldn't do it'. Not only the Claude default behaviour to try to figure this out always leads to wastage of tokens, it can also have very nasty side effects (which I had in the past) where a session with limited context (of the topic at hand) tries to do something about it, and then messes it up. This is usually called 'hallucinations', but is really a 'valid request given to an agent/llm who doesn't have enough (active) context to understand it' (I use 'active' in the root cause of the hallucination, because usually the context required to not-hallucinate was there at some point, but after a couple rounds of compression, it was removed)."
